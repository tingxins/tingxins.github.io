Practice flow

从导入到复习，围绕一句话完成闭环。

句练不把你带进复杂学习系统，只把本地音频训练这件事做清楚：找到一句、重复一句、保存一句、回看一句。

01Practice

逐句听说训练

打开音频后，从当前句子开始练。播放、暂停、回退和继续练习都围绕同一个训练现场，不需要在文件列表和播放器之间来回找。

  • 继续最近练习，减少重新查找时间。
  • 适合听写、跟读、影子练习和精听。
02Loop

A/B 片段复读

把难句的开始和结束点标出来，只重复那一小段。可以调整速度和复读次数，让耳朵真正跟上细节。

  • 精确复读短句、连读、停顿和语调。
  • 保存片段后，下次直接回到重点。
03Library

本地素材库

从 Files、AirDrop 或文件共享导入音频，整理成自己的训练材料。素材以本地库为中心，不把应用变成杂乱的文件浏览器。

  • 本地导入，本地管理。
  • 保留音频、片段和练习记录之间的连接。
04Review

收藏片段复习

把真正值得反复练的片段留下来。复习页帮你回到收藏和保存的句段，而不是重新听完整音频找重点。

  • 收藏片段和保存片段集中复习。
  • 让复读训练从一次播放变成持续积累。
05Settings

复读习惯自己定

默认复读次数、播放速度、导入后是否直接练习、本地记录清理和法律入口，都放在设置里清清楚楚。

  • 控制复读次数、倍速和导入行为。
  • 可以清除学习记录，并查看隐私政策与服务条款。

Local-first privacy

训练材料主要留在你的设备里。

句练的核心数据围绕本地音频、播放进度、A/B 标记、收藏片段和复习记录展开。产品分析只用于理解功能是否顺畅，并过滤音频内容、文件名、完整路径和原始搜索词。

FAQ

开始前你可能会问。

适合哪些材料？

适合播客、讲座音频、听力材料、口语跟读素材和自己收集的本地音频。

会替我管理学习计划吗？

不会。句练是一个本地听说训练工具，重点是导入音频、复读片段、保存重点和复习。

为什么强调 A/B 复读？

因为语言训练常常卡在几秒钟的连读、弱读、语调和停顿上。短片段重复比整段重听更有效。

隐私政策在哪里？

你可以查看 隐私政策 和 服务条款，了解数据处理和使用规则。

Practice flow

From import to review, built around the sentence you are practicing.

Vocer keeps the workflow focused: find a phrase, repeat it, save it, and come back when it is time to review.

01Practice

Practice one sentence at a time

Start from the current sentence and stay in the practice context. Playback, pause, rewind, and continue are all close to the material you are training on.

  • Resume recent practice without hunting through files.
  • Useful for dictation, shadowing, close listening, and speaking practice.
02Loop

Loop the exact phrase

Set A and B points around the hard phrase, then repeat just that moment. Adjust speed and repeat count until the details become clear.

  • Repeat short phrases, connected speech, pauses, and intonation.
  • Save segments so you can return directly to the important part.
03Library

Import audio your way

Bring in audio from Files, AirDrop, or file sharing, then keep it in a clean local materials library. Vocer stays centered on practice instead of becoming a generic file browser.

  • Local import and local material management.
  • Keep audio, segments, and learning records connected.
04Review

Review what matters

Save the clips that deserve another pass. The review page brings you back to favorite and saved segments instead of making you scan the full recording again.

  • Review favorite and saved clips in one place.
  • Turn repetition into a growing practice record.
05Settings

Personalize every repeat

Default repeat count, playback speed, import behavior, local data cleanup, and legal links are kept clear in Settings.

  • Control repeat count, speed, and import behavior.
  • Clear learning records and view the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Vocer is built around local audio, playback progress, A/B markers, saved clips, and review records. Product analytics are used only to understand whether features work smoothly, and app code filters audio content, file names, full paths, and raw search text.

FAQ

Before you start.

What materials work well?

Podcasts, lecture audio, listening materials, speaking practice files, and local recordings you already have the right to use.

Does Vocer manage study plans?

No. Vocer is a local listening and speaking practice tool focused on import, repeat loops, saved clips, and review.

Why focus on A/B repeat?

Language practice often breaks down in a few seconds of connected speech, weak forms, intonation, or pauses. Short-loop repetition is more focused than replaying an entire file.

Where is the privacy policy?

You can read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for data handling and use rules.
