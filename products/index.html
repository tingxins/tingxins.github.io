How they are made怎么做出来的

What they have in common. 它们的共同点。

  1. One job each.一款只做一件事。

    Every app here is built around a single task, and stops there. Nothing is added just to fill out a comparison table. 这里的每一款都围绕一件事来做，做完就停。不会为了填满功能对比表而堆东西。

  2. Native, so they feel quick.原生，所以够快。

    Written as iPhone apps rather than web pages in a wrapper. They launch straight into the thing you came to do. 按 iPhone 应用来写，而不是套壳网页。打开就直接进入你要做的那件事。

  3. Honest about limits.如实说明边界。

    When an app cannot tell you something, it says so plainly. Uncertainty is a result, not a gap to fill with a number. 当应用无法给出答案时，它就直说。不确定本身就是结论，而不是用一个数字去填的空白。

  4. Quiet by design.安静是设计的一部分。

    No ads, no streaks, no notifications inventing a reason to pull you back. You open them when you need them. 没有广告，没有打卡连胜，也没有为了把你拉回来而编出理由的通知。需要时你自然会打开。
