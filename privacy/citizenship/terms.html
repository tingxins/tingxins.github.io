What the app does

Citizenship reads provenance information that files carry — cryptographic Content Credentials (C2PA), plaintext declarations such as China's GB 45438 AIGC labels and IPTC metadata, and openly readable watermarks — and reports what it found, together with how much weight each finding carries.

The limits of a result — please read this part

A result describes a file. It never describes a person. The app makes no statement about who created something, whether anyone acted dishonestly, or whether a claim is true.

The app cannot determine whether content is AI-generated. It can only report evidence a file carries, and that evidence is frequently absent.

  • "No AI indicators found" is not a finding of authenticity. Most sharing, re-saving, and screenshotting strips provenance. An empty result is the expected outcome for a great many genuine and AI-generated files alike.
  • Declarations and watermarks can be forged or removed. Only the signed layer is tamper-evident, and even a valid signature proves who signed a file and that it has not changed since — not that what it depicts is real.
  • A camera signature does not prove what the camera was pointed at. A signed photograph of a screen is still a signed photograph.
  • Absence of a watermark proves nothing. The app reads only openly documented schemes; most commercial watermarks cannot be read by any third party.
  • Trust lists change. A signature considered valid today may be reported differently later if a certificate is revoked. Results are a snapshot of the moment they were made.

Do not use results as proof

Results are informational. They are not legal evidence, not a forensic examination, and not a basis on which to accuse anyone of anything. Do not rely on them for legal, journalistic, employment, academic-misconduct, safety, or financial decisions without independent verification. Where a matter is consequential, consult a qualified professional and obtain the original file from its source.

  • Only check files you have the right to access.
  • Do not present a result as something it is not — in particular, do not describe an empty result as confirmation that a file is genuine.
  • Do not use the app to harass, defame, or accuse.

本应用做什么

Citizenship 读取文件携带的溯源信息——加密内容凭证（C2PA）、明文声明（如中国 GB 45438 的 AIGC 标识与 IPTC 元数据）以及可公开读取的水印——并报告发现的内容及其分量。

结论的局限——请务必阅读

结论描述的是文件，绝不描述人。本应用不对内容由谁创作、 是否有人存在不诚实行为、或某项主张是否属实作出任何判断。

本应用无法判定内容是否由 AI 生成。 它只能报告文件所携带的证据，而这些证据经常是缺失的。

  • 「未发现 AI 痕迹」不等于认定内容真实。大多数分享、另存与截图都会剥离溯源信息。 对于大量真实以及 AI 生成的文件而言，空结果都是意料之中的。
  • 声明与水印可被伪造或移除。只有签名层可发现篡改； 而即使签名有效，也只能证明文件由谁签署、且此后未被修改——并不能证明其所呈现的内容属实。
  • 相机签名无法证明相机拍摄的对象。对着屏幕拍摄的照片，依然是一张有签名的照片。
  • 读不到水印不能说明任何问题。本应用只读取公开记录的方案； 多数商业水印无法被任何第三方读取。
  • 信任列表会变化。今天被判定有效的签名，若证书日后被吊销，结论可能不同。 结论只是作出当时的快照。

请勿将结论用作证据

结论仅供参考。它不是法律证据，不是司法鉴定，也不能作为指控他人的依据。 在未经独立核实的情况下，请勿据此作出法律、新闻、雇佣、学术不端、安全或财务方面的决定。 事关重大时，请咨询有资质的专业人士，并向来源方索取原始文件。
