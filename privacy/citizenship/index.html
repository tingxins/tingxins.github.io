What you see

Check a file from wherever you found it — the share sheet in Photos, Safari, a chat app, or Files. The result appears in place.

你会看到什么

在你发现文件的任何地方检测它——「照片」、Safari、聊天应用或「文件」中的分享面板。 结果就地显示。

Five layers of evidence

Every finding belongs to a layer, and the lower the layer, the less it can say. The app shows the layer on every line.

五个证据层级

每条证据都属于某一层级，层级越低，能说明的问题越少。应用会在每一行标注层级。

◈
Signed签名 Cryptographic provenance (C2PA Content Credentials) — verifiable and tamper-evident. 加密溯源（C2PA 内容凭证）——可验证、可发现篡改。
◇
Declared声明 Plaintext declarations (GB 45438 AIGC labels, IPTC). Trustworthy in origin, but forgeable and removable. 明文声明（GB 45438 的 AIGC 标识、IPTC）。来源可信，但可伪造、可移除。
≈
Watermark水印 Openly readable watermarks (Adobe TrustMark, Meta AudioSeal). Absence proves nothing. 可公开读取的水印（Adobe TrustMark、Meta AudioSeal）。读不到并不代表没有。
≀
Inference推断 Statistical model output — a lead, never evidence. Not currently used: no model has met our accuracy gates. 统计模型输出——只是线索，不是证据。目前未启用：尚无模型通过我们的准确率门槛。
○
Context上下文 File-shape clues (screenshot, re-encode). Explains why evidence is missing; never accuses. 文件形态线索（截图、转码）。用来解释证据为何缺失，绝不用于指控。

What it will never do

  • No accuracy percentages. There isn't one, anywhere.
  • No guessing at text. Documents are read for declared provenance only — statistical "AI writing" detection systematically misflags non-native writers, so it is permanently excluded.
  • No accusations. A result describes a file, never a person.
  • No claim a file was cropped. When an image is too small to check, it says exactly that.

它绝不会做的事

  • 不显示准确率百分比。应用中任何地方都没有。
  • 不猜测文本。文档只读取声明式溯源——统计式「AI 写作」检测会系统性误判非母语写作者，因此被永久排除。
  • 不做指控。结论描述的是文件，绝不是人。
  • 不断言文件被裁剪。当图片太小无法检测时，应用就直接这么说。

Checking is entirely on-device. The file you check is never uploaded — there is no account and no server that holds your content. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (which screens are opened, what kind of result a check produced) and nothing else. See the privacy policy.

检测完全在设备本机完成。你检测的文件绝不会被上传 ——没有账号，也没有任何服务器保存你的内容。应用只发送匿名的使用统计 （打开了哪些界面、检测得到哪一类结论），除此之外没有别的。详见 隐私政策。
