Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 August 2026

Citizenship collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. The app makes no network requests at all — the files you check never leave your device, because there is nowhere for them to go.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays in the app's own storage on your device. It is included in your device backups if you have those enabled, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

DataWhyYour control
Scan history — the verdict, the evidence list, a small thumbnail, and the time of the check So you can look back at a previous result Settings ▸ Keep history. Turning it off deletes what was stored, including thumbnails. History ▸ Clear removes it at any time.
Two preferences — whether onboarding was completed, and the keep-history setting So the app behaves the way you left it Removed with the app
Temporary copies of a file being checked Some formats must be read from disk (video hashing, documents) Replaced on the next scan and cleared by the system; cancelling a scan deletes the copy immediately

What the app accesses while you use it

  • Photos you pick. Selection uses Apple's system photo picker, which runs outside the app. Citizenship receives only the item you chose and never gets access to your photo library — which is why it never asks for photo permission.
  • Files you choose or share. Only the specific file you select, or the one you send in via the share sheet.
  • The clipboard, only when you tap Paste. The app checks whether the clipboard holds an image or text so it can enable or grey out the Paste row; it reads the contents only when you explicitly tap it.

The share extension behaves the same way, and writes no history at all.

What is never collected

No personal information, no contact details, no location, no device or advertising identifiers, no usage or crash analytics, no diagnostics. Nothing is shared with any third party, because nothing is transmitted anywhere. The app's App Store privacy manifest declares zero collected data types and no tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If a future version ever transmits anything — for example an optional cloud check — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask for your explicit consent each time, on a per-scan basis. No such feature exists today.

Contact

Questions about this policy: citizenship@midaigc.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 7 日

Citizenship 不收集任何关于你的数据。没有账号、 没有数据分析、没有广告、没有追踪，也没有服务器。应用不发起任何网络请求—— 你检测的文件不会离开设备，因为它根本无处可去。

应用存储什么，存在哪里

以下内容全部保存在设备上应用自己的存储空间中。若你启用了设备备份，它们会包含在备份内； 删除应用时会一并删除。

数据用途你的控制方式
检测历史——结论、证据列表、一张小缩略图与检测时间 方便你回看此前的结果 设置 ▸ 保留历史记录。关闭即删除已存储内容（含缩略图）。历史记录 ▸ 清除可随时清空。
两项偏好设置——是否已完成引导，以及是否保留历史记录 让应用保持你上次的使用状态 随应用一并删除
被检测文件的临时副本 部分格式必须从磁盘读取（视频哈希、文档） 下次扫描时替换，并由系统清理；取消扫描会立即删除该副本

使用期间应用会访问什么

  • 你选择的照片。选择过程使用 Apple 的系统照片选择器，它在应用之外运行。 Citizenship 只会收到你选中的那一项，永远无法访问你的照片图库——这也是它从不索取照片权限的原因。
  • 你选择或分享的文件。仅限你选中的那个文件，或你通过分享面板发送进来的文件。
  • 剪贴板，且仅在你点按「粘贴」时。应用会检查剪贴板中是否有图片或文本， 以便决定「粘贴」一行是可用还是置灰；只有在你明确点按时才会读取其内容。

分享扩展的行为完全相同，并且不写入任何历史记录。

绝不收集的内容

不收集个人信息、联系方式、位置、设备或广告标识符、使用或崩溃分析、诊断数据。 不会与任何第三方共享，因为没有任何内容被传输出去。应用的 App Store 隐私清单 声明了零收集数据类型且无追踪。

儿童

本应用并非面向儿童，且不收集任何人的任何数据，无论年龄。

政策变更

如果未来版本会传输任何内容——例如可选的云端检测——本政策会在该版本发布前更新， 并且应用会在每次扫描时单独征求你的明确同意。目前不存在此类功能。
